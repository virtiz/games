Holdfast

Red Banner · Black Ops

1v1 soldier card duel. Reduce the enemy commander to 0 HP to win. Play vs a bot on a phone-sized stage. Choose your side:

Red Banner

Durable infantry, Guards, heals, and midrange pressure. Hold the line, then grind them down.

Black Ops

Aggressive strikers, pings, draw, and burst Orders. Race the enemy commander.

Co-op = you + ally vs 30 HP Siege Titan. Hotseat modes hide hands between turns.

How to play

  • Both commanders start at 20 HP. Drop theirs to 0.
  • Supply rises by 1 each turn (max 10) and refills. Spend it to play cards.
  • Deploy Soldiers (max 5). They cannot attack the turn they arrive. Orders resolve instantly.
  • Attack enemy soldiers or the commander. If they have a Guard, you must hit a Guard first.

Holdfast rules

Goal: Reduce the enemy commander to 0 HP. That is the only win condition.

Supply: Capacity rises by 1 each turn (max 10) and refills. First turn starts at 1. Skip draw on your very first turn only.

Soldiers: Deploy to your single field row (max 5). Summoning sickness — cannot attack the turn played. Wake at the start of your turn.

Orders: Instant effects (damage, heal, draw, buff). Then discarded.

Guard: If any enemy Guard is on the field, you must attack a Guard before other soldiers or the commander.

Combat: Soldiers deal Attack to each other. Destroy at 0 HP. Direct attack deals Attack to the enemy commander.

Fatigue: Empty deck draw deals escalating damage to your commander.

Controls: Drag a playable card onto your field (or tap). Tap a glowing soldier to attack — preview shows the outcome before you commit.
